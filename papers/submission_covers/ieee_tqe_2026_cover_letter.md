# Cover Letter — IEEE Transactions on Quantum Engineering

**Date**: 2026-07-30
**To**: The Editor, *IEEE Transactions on Quantum Engineering*
**From**: TOE-SYLVA Standards Committee
**Subject**: Submission of "Standardized Benchmark Suite for Entanglement-Geometry Algorithms: EGSF-20"

Dear Editor,

We submit our manuscript presenting **EGSF-20**, a standardized benchmark suite of 20 canonical problems for testing entanglement-geometry algorithms. Developed under the Entanglement Geometry Standardization Forum (EGSF), the suite covers Ising models, SYK, Kitaev chains, BTZ black holes, AdS₃/CFT₂, cMERA RG, Page curves, surface codes, OTOC, wormhole decoding, topological ML, cold-atom SYK, cMERA-LES, brain entropy, M87* shadow, Sgr A* ripples, 4-MZM braiding, and QNN gradients.

Key contributions:
1. **Standardized API** (`sylva-core` v1.0) with 5 core functions
2. **7 participating institutions** (IBM, Microsoft, Origin-Q, Google, MPI, CAS, TOE-SYLVA)
3. **.sylva HDF5 data format** specification
4. **Reproducibility protocol** (Docker + Zenodo DOI + seeded RNG)
