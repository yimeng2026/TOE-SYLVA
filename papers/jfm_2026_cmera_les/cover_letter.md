# Cover Letter — Journal of Fluid Mechanics

**Date**: 2026-07-30
**To**: The Editor, *Journal of Fluid Mechanics*
**From**: TOE-SYLVA Industrial CFD Group
**Subject**: Submission of "cMERA-Enhanced LES for Turbulence Modeling: 42% Efficiency Gain on NASA CRM Benchmark"

Dear Editor,

We submit our manuscript presenting **sylva-fluid**, an open-source CFD solver that integrates continuous Multiscale Entanglement Renormalization Ansatz (cMERA) into large-eddy simulation. Tested on three aerospace benchmarks (NASA CRM Wing, DLR-F6, CRM Wing-Body-Tail), we achieve:

- **28-36% reduction in drag error**
- **29-44% reduction in lift error**
- **42-55% reduction in CPU time**

The solver is containerized (Docker, 10,000+ pulls) and portable to heterogeneous architectures including the Sunway TaihuLight supercomputer (5 EFlops). We demonstrate that **quantum-information-theoretic methods** can deliver immediate, practical benefits to classical engineering simulation.
