## §2 选者原理：从人择到因果稳定

$$P(\text{vacuum}) \propto \exp\left(\lambda \cdot L_{\max} - \sum_{L>1} \frac{\|\delta\mathcal{C}^{(L)}\|}{\|\mathcal{C}^{(L)}\|}\right)$$
