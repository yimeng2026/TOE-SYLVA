(\pnum{30}+\pnum{44}+\pnum{31}+\pnum{29}+\pnum{30}+\pnum{32}),

The reversal had an operational consequence beyond bookkeeping: the
draft TrivialBench sampling protocol (A-2) filtered modules by the

The protocol clause has been revised to the code-level caliber with
an explicit quarantine exception for the single genuine case.

\subsection{Quarantine case: \texttt{SYLVA\_ProvenLayer2.lean}}
\label{sec:quarantine}
The one genuine case concentrates all three irony modes of AI
generation. The file's header asserts it contains ``10 theorems with
ACTUAL PROOFS. No \texttt{True := trivial} placeholders --- all proofs
are real.'' In fact \pnum{8} of its \pnum{10} theorems are
\texttt{True := trivial}; one of the two remaining is named
\texttt{sylva\_zero\_sorry\_guarantee} --- the zero-sorry guarantee,
proved by \texttt{trivial} --- which made the file the only
non-declaration string-caliber \texttt{sorry} hit in the entire batch
corpus. Under the quarantine protocol the file was moved out of the
batch corpus into an isolation directory with a machine-readable
manifest (origin path, SHA-256, reason list, exclusion scope), the
audit report's status was updated, and the caliber impact was
measured rather than assumed: the static dashboard is untouched
(dashboards are generated artifacts, not live queries), and the next
regeneration will see the batch pool shrink from \pnum{119859} to
\pnum{119858} files with \pnum{10} template theorems removed from
both numerator and denominator of the batch caliber --- the intended
effect of quarantining a contamination source.

\subsection{Lifecycle case study: the Chern--Simons chain}
\label{sec:cschain}
% source: framework/chern_simons_theorem_report.md + D1 review 2.4;
% proof_status.md sec 3 registration rows.
The strongest single narrative in the repository is the staged
CLAIM$\to$THEOREM upgrade along the Chern--Simons dependency chain:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Claim identification}: the fine-structure coincidence
$\alpha^{-1}\!\approx\!n_{CS}\!=\!137$ is registered as a CLAIM with
an explicit falsifiability criterion (first-principles prediction of
$\alpha^{-1}$ to $\pm 0.01$, independent of loop-integer self-tuning).
\item \textbf{Chain census}: the 8-file Chern--Simons side-chain was
inventoried with per-file placeholder/axiom census, exposing exactly
which links of the purported derivation were load-bearing debt.
\item \textbf{Deepest provable link upgraded}: the stratified
Chern-number identity in the integer-weight sector
(\texttt{stratifiedChernNumber\_int\_weights}) was formalized via
mathlib delegation (\texttt{Int.cast\_sum}, \texttt{Int.cast\_mul}),
independently compiled (exit 0, \pnum{6.7}\,s), with
\texttt{\#print axioms} reduced to the standard trio.
% baseline: 6.7s compile (chern_simons_theorem_report.md)
\item \textbf{Honest labeling}: the upgraded statement is labeled what
it is --- a $\mathbb{Z}$-ring-closure identity in the integer-weight
sector, \emph{not} the topological integrality of the real-weight
Chern--Simons functional; that stronger statement remains open, and
the remaining gap chain (Chern--Weil theory absent from mathlib; the
spectral-action bridge axiom; the level placeholder
$\equiv\!137$) is registered alongside it.
\item \textbf{Registration}: the tier assignment and its evidence
live in the central registry (\texttt{proof\_status.md} sec 3), not
in the paper draft --- the draft cites the registry.
\end{enumerate}
The Dedekind unique-factorization sub-case demonstrates the other
half of the discipline: a delegation wrapper around mathlib's
\texttt{normalized\_factor\_uniq} proves a THEOREM with \emph{zero
new mathematics}, and the registry says so explicitly, rather than
letting the tier label imply novelty.
